# 0002 — Technology stack

- **Status:** Proposed
- **Date:** 2026-09-04

## Context

No technology stack has been chosen for FreshKeep, and none should be chosen
yet. The stack is a consequence of what the project needs to do, so it follows
from the [charter](../project-charter.md) and the
[requirements](../requirements.md) rather than preceding them. Picking a stack
first and then discovering the requirements it cannot meet is expensive
precisely when there is least time left.

The questions that will decide it:

- **What form does the client take** — web, mobile, or both? This constrains
  more than anything else on the list.
- **Is a server needed?** Is there persistent, multi-user or shared data, or
  can everything run on-device and store locally?
- **Are any technologies required or excluded by the programme?** Check the
  assessment brief before evaluating anything else; a constraint here removes
  options for free.
- **What can realistically be built, tested and demonstrated in the time
  available?** A stack that could produce a better system in six months is the
  wrong stack for a deadline that is sooner than that.

**Familiarity is an explicit constraint, not an afterthought.** An unfamiliar
stack spends schedule on learning rather than on building, and that spend has
to buy something real — a requirement the familiar option genuinely cannot
meet, or a capability the assessment explicitly rewards. "It is what people
use" is not a reason. Neither is "it would look good on a CV", at the cost of
a working submission.
